Title: Instagram and Facebook Subscriptions Get New Scrutiny in Child Safety Suit

Publication date: 2024-02-27
URL: https://www.nytimes.com/2024/02/26/us/instagram-facebook-children-subscriptions-predators.html

Full text: The New Mexico attorney general, who last year sued Meta alleging that it did not protect children from sexual predators and had made false claims about its platforms’ safety, announced Monday that his office would examine how the company’s paid-subscription services attract predators.
